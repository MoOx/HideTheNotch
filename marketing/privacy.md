# Privacy Policy

Hide The Notch is made by Maxime Thirouin. It has no account, no ads, no
analytics and no tracking. It sends one thing, to one place, and only when
something goes wrong.

This describes the code rather than an intention: the app is open source, and
every claim below can be checked against it.

## Your photos are not collected

The system photo picker hands the app the one image you chose and nothing else.
It is decoded, drawn and exported on the device. Nothing about it is uploaded,
because the app has no server. A wallpaper you export is written to your photo
library, and only when you ask for it.

## Crash reports

When the app fails it sends a crash report to [Sentry](https://sentry.io), over
an encrypted connection. Sentry is the only third party that receives anything,
and the reports are used to fix bugs and for nothing else. Nothing is sold, and
nothing is shared with anyone else.

**In the report**: the error and where it happened in the code, the app version,
the device model and operating system version, and a random identifier generated
at installation so that several reports from one phone can be recognised as the
same phone. That identifier is not linked to you, is not an advertising
identifier, and is not used to follow you anywhere.

**Deliberately not in it**, each turned off in the app's own source: your IP
address, screen recordings, performance measurements, and the app's own log
messages.

## Keeping and deleting

Crash reports are kept for 90 days and then deleted automatically. To have
earlier ones removed, write to the address below and say so.

## Permissions

Your photo library, so you can pick a photo and save a wallpaper. On Android the
app asks for the narrowest permission that allows a single pick and a single
write rather than for the whole library. Nothing else is requested: no location,
no contacts, no microphone.
